# UX research plan

> Output of `/ux-research-planner`, Jun 5, 2026. The original Google Doc is linked in [links.md](../links.md). Example data.

## Research question

How do partner users look for a template today, and where do they give up?

## Method

- 5 remote interviews with partner campaign managers, 30 minutes each
- Task: "Find a WhatsApp welcome template and open it"

## Participants

| # | Partner type | Templates used per month |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Retail | 10 to 20 |
| P2 | Travel | 5 to 10 |
| P3 | Agency (4 partners) | 40+ |
| P4 | Retail | 10 to 20 |
| P5 | Finance | Under 5 |
