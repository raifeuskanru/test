# Not chosen, and why

> Alternatives that were dropped and decisions that were postponed, with their reasons. Claude asks for these at every stage end. Example data.

## Left sidebar with all filters

- **Stage:** pre-design, ideation option A
- **Status:** dropped
- **Why:** it took a third of the screen on 13-inch laptops, where most partners work. The benchmark showed the same problem for Competitor B.
- **When, who:** Jun 16, 2026, Product Designer A with the PM

## One "Filter" button opening a panel

- **Stage:** pre-design, ideation option C
- **Status:** dropped
- **Why:** it hides the filters. In research, users scanned the page before they searched.
- **When, who:** Jun 16, 2026, Product Designer A
