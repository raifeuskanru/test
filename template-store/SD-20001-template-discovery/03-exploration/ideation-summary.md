# Ideation summary

> Summary of the ui-ideation output, Jun 14, 2026. The three options are clickable in [artifacts/ideation](../artifacts/ideation/). Example data.

| Option | Idea | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A | Left sidebar with all filters | Dropped: takes a third of the screen on 13-inch laptops |
| **B** | **Horizontal chips above the grid** | **Chosen**: keeps the grid wide, matches research finding 1 |
| C | One "Filter" button opening a panel | Dropped: hides the filters, research showed users scan before they search |

Copy for all options came from the UX Writing Library, so the comparison was about layout, not wording.
